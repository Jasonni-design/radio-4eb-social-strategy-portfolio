01 — PROJECT

A strategy to help more people find the programmes already on air.

Radio 4EB is Brisbane's multilingual community radio station, serving more than 50 language groups. The team project examined its social channels and developed a practical pilot that reuses programme material instead of relying on a high-volume production schedule.

The report is a proposed strategy, not a record of implementation or achieved growth.

01 / AUDIENCE

Read the evidence with care.

Compared Australian age-group radio use (2024) with selected social platform use (2024/25), then translated the context into practical listening guidance and account-discovery recommendations.

National context ≠ 4EB audience data↗
02 / COMPETITORS

Find repeatable ideas, not vanity metrics.

Reviewed public examples from 4ZZZ, SBS and Triple A Murri. The analysis focused on visible formats, branding and listening paths—not unverifiable publishing cadence or performance.

Three peer organisations reviewed↗
